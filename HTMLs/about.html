About the SPAP System

The Student Placement Analytics & Placement Prediction System (SPAP) is designed to transform traditional campus recruitment workflows into data-informed, proactive placement strategies. By analyzing academic histories, technical evaluation scores, aptitude benchmarks, internships, and work experience, the system provides automated placement forecasting and personalized skill enhancement guidance.
